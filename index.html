1. About Kana Labs

Kana Labs (now evolving under Xyra Labs) is an all-in-one Web3 middleware and infrastructure platform. It acts as an aggregator of experiences, connecting communities across multiple blockchains (EVM and non-EVM networks like Solana, Aptos, Sui, and Ethereum) into a secure, intuitive, and high-performance interface.

By abstracting technical complexities, Kana Labs empowers both retail users and developers with institutional-grade tooling, cross-chain bridging, and next-generation decentralized applications.

2. Core Products & Offerings

Web3 Aggregator SDK (Kana Crosschain SDK)

Enables crypto dApps to expand from single-chain to multi-chain deployments instantly. It combines proprietary liquidity aggregation engines, cross-chain asset bridges, and smart routing algorithms to optimize transaction pricing and execution.

Mirai SDK (Smart Wallet & Paymaster)

A self-custody smart wallet powered by Account Abstraction (AA). Features include email/social login for seamless Web2-to-Web3 onboarding, gasless transactions, transaction batching (one-click multi-calls), and cross-chain bridging.

Kana Perps (Perpetual Futures DEX)

A 100% on-chain, Central Limit Order Book (CLOB) based perpetual futures platform supporting advanced trading modes (such as Hedge Mode) with massive transaction volumes and high throughput.

3. Key Technological Benefits

  • Multi-Chain Interoperability: Seamlessly bridge and transfer assets across diverse blockchain architectures without fragmentation.
  • Optimized Smart Routing: Automated liquidity aggregation across decentralized exchanges to minimize slippage and transaction costs.
  • Gasless Experience: Pay gas fees using any token or let applications sponsor transactions via Paymaster integration.
  • Enterprise-Grade Security: Non-custodial framework placing absolute asset control securely in the hands of the end user.
